Our Heritage

Where Culinary Artistry Meets Architectural Minimalism

"Food should excite the eyes before it pleases the palate. We treat every plate as a fresh canvas."

Nestled on a breezy, beautifully lit rooftop in Sector 1, HSR Layout, Casa Fresco was born out of a desire to blend Mediterranean seaside charm with contemporary global gastronomy. Our open-air dining room acts as an urban escape—a calm oasis where Bengaluru's fast-paced crowd can unwind under the sky.

Every ingredient, from the San Marzano tomatoes on our thin-crust pizzas to the winter truffles in our hand-rolled fettuccine, is sourced directly from ethical local farms and premium Italian suppliers. Here, we honor slow cooking methods, bringing you rich, authentic flavors cooked with passion and served with luxury style.

The Visionary

Crafting Breathtaking Stories On a Plate

"True fine dining is not just about satiety—it is sensory theater. The smell of burning wood, the gloss of olive oil, the view of the open sky. We curate all of it."

Giovanni Rossi trained under double Michelin-star kitchens in Florence before moving to India to lead the culinary transition at Casa Fresco. He approaches recipe design with a deep respect for Italian techniques, combined with premium global flavor profiles.

At Casa Fresco, he introduces curated culinary tasting dinners, seasonal ingredient-first pairings, and teaches the art of handcrafted dough throwing to our master bakers.
