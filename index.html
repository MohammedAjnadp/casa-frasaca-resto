Our Heritage

The Casa Fresco Experience

Where culinary artistry meets architectural minimalism.

Nestled on a breezy rooftop in HSR Layout, Casa Fresco blends Mediterranean seaside elegance with contemporary global fare. Our space is designed as an urban oasis—a retreat from Bengaluru’s bustle where you can indulge in wood-fired pizzas, handcrafted house pastas, and artisanal coffee under the open sky.

Every recipe is developed in-house using premium locally-sourced produce and fine imported cheeses to transport you straight to the Italian coast.
